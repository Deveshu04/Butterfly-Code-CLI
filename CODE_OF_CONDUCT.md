# Code of Conduct

Butterfly Code adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. Everyone who participates in the project's spaces
(issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) is expected to follow it.

## In short

- Be respectful and considerate. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- No harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language or unwelcome attention.
- Assume good faith and help newcomers.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers by opening a
[private security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) titled
"Code of conduct report", or by contacting a maintainer listed in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove content and restrict participation, following the
enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
